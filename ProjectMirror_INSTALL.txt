================================================================================
INTEGRATSIYA V MAIN.BB:
================================================================================

1. INCLUDE (posle stroki ~2688, gde ostalnye Include):
-------------------------------------------------------

Include "ProjectMirror_Core.bb"


2. INIT (pered stroki ~8542, v funktsii InitNewGame ili podobnoy):
------------------------------------------------------------------

InitProjectMirror()


3. UPDATE (posle stroki ~2989, v glavnom game loop):
----------------------------------------------------

UpdateProjectMirror()


4. RENDER (pered strokou ~3355, v HUD rendering):
-------------------------------------------------

RenderProjectMirror()


5. CLEANUP (v funktsii vyhoda/novoy igry):
------------------------------------------

CleanupProjectMirror()


6. SAVE/LOAD (v funktsiyakh sohraneniya/zagruzki):
--------------------------------------------------

; v Save:
SaveProjectMirrorState(file)

; v Load:
LoadProjectMirrorState(file)


7. HOOKS (optsional'no, dlya polnoy integratsii):
-------------------------------------------------

; Kogda igrok beret predmet:
OnMirrorItemPickup(item)

; Kogda sozdaetsya NPC:
OnMirrorNPCCreated(npc)

; Kogda igrok vhodit v komnatu:
OnMirrorRoomEnter(room)

; Kogda igraetsya zvuk dialoga:
OnMirrorDialogPlayed(soundPath, speakerName, room)
